Mako

Mako was stolen from the rich upper city as a baby and raised in the broken one below. I wrote his story first, then built him: spiky hair, round shades, a face mask and a high collar to hide behind. His workshop is full of salvaged engines and cyan blueprints, with the golden towers of the city he came from glowing through the window.
